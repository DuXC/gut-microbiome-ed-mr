Please consider our Article, “Bidirectional Mendelian randomization of gut microbiota and erectile dysfunction with independent replication and multiplicity control,” for publication in the *International Journal of Impotence Research*.

Multiple recent MR reports, including an Article in IJIR, have proposed causal gut microbial taxa for erectile dysfunction. The present study addresses the key reproducibility question using a materially updated evidence base: species-resolved shotgun-metagenomic GWASs from 16,017 Swedish participants, exact-label independent exposure validation in up to 12,652 HUNT participants, FinnGen R12, and the 2025 multi-ancestry erectile-dysfunction GWAS. Eligibility was frozen before association screening, instruments were genome-wide significant and ancestry-clumped, forward and reverse false-discovery-rate families were controlled separately, and known sample overlap was not counted as replication.

No association survived multiplicity control in either direction. The two nominal forward traits with exact HUNT matches also failed validation. We believe this carefully bounded negative result is important for IJIR readers because it directly tests a rapidly expanding therapeutic narrative, shows why nominal taxon-level signals should not yet guide microbiome intervention for erectile dysfunction, and provides a reproducible standard for future MR studies in sexual medicine. The manuscript does not claim proof of no effect and explicitly identifies the limits created by sparse microbial instruments and incomplete taxonomic matching.

This work is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the manuscript and its submission. The authors declare no competing interests. The study used only publicly available, deidentified GWAS summary statistics and required no new ethical approval or participant consent.
