# IJIR v0.3 figure contract

Core conclusion: No association met the predefined multiplicity-controlled criteria for a robust causal interpretation, while sparse instruments and minimum detectable effects show that small or feature-specific effects remain unresolved.

Figure archetype: Schematic-led Figure 1; quantitative-grid Figures 2 and 3; compact quantitative supplementary figures.

Target journal/output: International Journal of Impotence Research; double-column 183 mm artwork; R-only PDF, SVG, TIFF, 600 dpi PNG and 300 dpi QA preview.

Backend: R.

Final size: Main figures 183 mm wide; heights 86–142 mm. Supplementary figures 183 mm wide; heights 92–135 mm.

Panel map:

- Figure 1: forward design and evidence levels; reverse design; arrow-key evidence hierarchy.
- Figure 2: forward and reverse ranked P values against frozen-family BH, global Bonferroni and nominal thresholds.
- Figure 3a: seven nominal Swedish Wald estimates with the nested identical Peptococcaceae cluster marked.
- Figure 3b: same-SNP Swedish–HUNT signed-Z comparison for the two nominal exact-label traits.
- Figure 3c: forward IV-count architecture and 80% power minimum detectable OR.

Evidence hierarchy:

- Hero evidence: primary forward and reverse frozen-family multiplicity results.
- Validation evidence: exact-label, same-SNP HUNT exposure-association validation; not independent MR replication.
- Controls/robustness: estimable-only and unique-cluster multiplicity, source-study thresholds, known-overlap outcomes, power and bounded mechanistic screening.

Statistics needed: OR and 95% CI, nominal P, BH q, family denominators, exact binomial direction-concordance CI, R² and mRnd minimum detectable OR, planned/estimable/nominal/FDR counts.

Source data needed: frozen primary forward and reverse results plus the code-generated v0.3 HUNT, power, multiplicity, source-threshold and mechanistic files.

Image-integrity notes: No external imagery; no selective removal; all plotted rows come from exported source-data CSVs. Line widths are at least 1 pt, and previews are generated by R.

Reviewer risk: Same-SNP direction concordance is not equivalent to MR replication; presence and abundance scales differ; 89% of estimable forward traits use one SNP; known-overlap 2025 outcomes are sensitivity evidence; multiplicity-adjusted MDEs are large.
