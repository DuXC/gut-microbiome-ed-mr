# Publication-path addendum: transparent negative report

Date: 2026-07-20

The frozen `NO-GO` decision in `analysis_decisions.md` remains unchanged for a
positive causal manuscript. No threshold, eligibility rule, multiplicity
family, replication gate, estimator, or result is reopened by this addendum.

Following completion of the prespecified analysis, the lead author authorized a
separate publication path for a transparent negative report. That report may
describe the complete bidirectional analysis because the study provides a
methodological and data increment over earlier gut microbiota–erectile
dysfunction Mendelian-randomization reports: shotgun-metagenomic exposure GWAS,
exact-label independent HUNT exposure validation, FinnGen R12, the 2025
multi-ancestry erectile-dysfunction GWAS as a known-overlap sensitivity source,
ancestry-matched LD clumping, direction-specific false-discovery-rate control,
and a hash-bound reproducibility record.

Publication language must preserve the following boundary:

- no nominal association is described as causal, replicated, protective, or
  harmful;
- the seven nominal forward results and 77 nominal reverse results remain
  explicitly non-significant after multiplicity control;
- the report may conclude that the analysis found no robust evidence under the
  tested data and assumptions, but it may not claim proof of no causal effect;
- raw third-party GWAS files are not redistributed unless their source terms
  permit it.
